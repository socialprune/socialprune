# CWOS Core Rule

> **MANDATORY FOR NON-TRIVIAL DELEGATED `.kilo` WORK**
> Preserve structured protocol data, pass-forward knowledge, and auditability without rebuilding a rule warehouse.
> **Version:** 1.1.0
> **Updated:** 2026-04-19

---

## Constitutional Minimum

CWOS survives in `.kilo` as a compressed constitutional core.

---

## Hard Laws

1. Non-trivial delegated work must produce structured protocol or handoff data.
2. Knowledge discovered in one lane must be passable to later lanes.
3. Orchestrated work must remain auditable.
4. Fresh-context artifact handoff remains the default posture for delegated work.
5. Procedural detail such as exact templates, log mechanics, and audit formatting belongs outside this constitutional rule.
6. Non-trivial delegated lanes use the operational companion for status vocabulary, compact artifact packaging, and anti-nested-recursion enforcement.

---

## Required CWOS Guarantees

### Protocol guarantee
Delegated work must leave enough structured evidence to show:
- what was attempted,
- what was decided,
- what was verified,
- and what remains blocked or deferred.

### Knowledge-transfer guarantee
Important decisions, discoveries, and warnings may not stay trapped in one lane's private context.

### Auditability guarantee
A later reviewer must be able to reconstruct the orchestration path from explicit artifacts rather than narrative memory.

---

## Boundaries

This rule preserves the constitutional minimum only.

Operational details for delegated-lane status vocabulary, compact `task / status / handoff / convergence` packaging, topology labels, and anti-nested-recursion enforcement live in:

- `.kilo/rules/cwos-operational-companion.md`

It does **not** attempt to encode:
- detailed section templates,
- subtask-log file mechanics,
- orchestration creativity playbooks,
- or specialized audit workflows.

---

## Failure Rule

If delegated work cannot be reconstructed, cannot pass forward key knowledge, or leaves no structured protocol trace, CWOS has been violated.
